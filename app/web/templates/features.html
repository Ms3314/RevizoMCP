Features

Revizo's MCP server is the main product — the coach lives inside the LLM you already use. The web dashboard is just a quiet companion view of the same data.

What to revise today

Your due list, ranked by how overdue it is, how hard it hit you, and whether you last failed it. Ask "what should I revise today?" and start.

Your own mistakes, reminded

Before every revisit the coach replays your notes in your own words — "you did an off-by-one here last time — check your loop bounds" — so you re-fight the exact spot you lost, not a generic version of the problem.

Weak patterns across everything

The coach aggregates your most common failure patterns and warns you before new problems, not just old ones. Your vocabulary:

{% for tag in ["complexity_tle", "edge_cases", "off_by_one", "overflow", "misread_constraints", "wrong_ds", "math_error", "implementation_bug"] %} {{ tag }} {% endfor %}

Rescheduled on truth

Success moves a problem further out on its difficulty ladder; failure brings it back tomorrow carrying the mistakes forward. Honest records make the schedule honest.

Real LeetCode sync

Pulls your real solves — and your recent failed attempts — via the public API, so the coach knows exactly what you're stuck on. For code diagnosis, paste your code in chat.

Works where you work

Cursor, Claude Desktop, Claude Code, ChatGPT — anything that speaks MCP. See the connect guides.
